===============================

welcome to my absolutely garbage mod for SPD.

do you hate yourself?
do you hate crabs and snakes?
do you hate rng biting in your ass?
or do you think vanilla is too easy?... (somehow)

well, i gotchu.


a few (bad) changes (to name a few):
-goo dropping living blobs of itself during the fight
-slimes having even more damage reduction than they already had
-snakes having even more dodge than before... because that's the one reason they exist
-thieves being able to steal equipped items
-DM-100 being smart by taking proper distance

and of course... all gear and your hero is nerfed ontop of all that, including starting with no armor!
but don't think that none of it isn't in your favor, there are quite a few proper positives too:
-hero's start with +10 hp
-strength potions give +2 strength instead of +1
-thieves being a bit slower
-crabs no longer being twice as fast
-and much much more

but in all honesty? expect to die alot...
oh, and don't even think about picking huntress solely for the spirit bow, because that's been nerfed obviously.


all in all: have fun, pray that you survive, and... don't be stupid :)
(note: while the mod does have all the vanilla translations, I will only ever change or add to the english translation)


general advice (if you died 100+ times and never got past jails, somehow):
1. don't overestimate yourself: in vanilla, maybe you should. but trust me, a albino rat is straight up death, and fighting the floor 2/3 quest enemy is a no-go unless you got some gear
2. play safe, and don't rush: whenever you fight a enemy, always try to fight it in a doorway, peferably a doorway of a room you are certain is safe. don't dash to a doorway of a new room, you'll be surrounded from both sides! in that same way, take on one enemy at a time if possible. no unnecessary risks! and besides: you get way more food than you need, you got plenty of time.
3. unlike vanilla, luck matters: getting a good tier 2 weapon and armour by floor 3 will show to be a great run, easily capable of entering the jails, and having the chance to beat floor 10. but if you can't find any armour or weapon by floor 4 will show to be a unlucky run where you'll very certainly die. in other words: be lucky enough to get what you need.
4. use your class!: you can pick a class, and should use its strength. if you go rogue, don't forget to use the cloak. and don't pick a class because 'it has this one item i think is really good', no. pick a class because you will go along with the very playstyle of that class. not doing so will lower your chances to survive.
5. don't greed: something you'll eventually fall into is greed. 'imagine if i defeat the living statue... i'd be set for the next 6 floors', or 'that is a mimic, and those always drop good loot!'. general advice: avoid scenario's like these completely. these have been buffed up to a point where you won't win unless you got good gear. if you have good gear, you don't need to kill them for more good gear. you can always still try and win in some cases, but expect to lose!

about: mod structure and modding this mod

this mod is made using the source code of vanilla SPD. it's exactly as you might expect: exactly SPD but with a few changes. internally, the code remains mostly the same with next to no big changes. also meaning that this is not dynamic, because vanilla isn't either.

if you can mod vanilla SPD, then you can certainly mod this mod too if you wish (for whatever reason). if you don't know how to mod vanilla SPD, you might want to read the 'readme' on their source code and go from there. you will likely need some java knowledge.
